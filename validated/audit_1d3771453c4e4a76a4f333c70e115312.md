### Title
Unpermissioned `request_refund` Allows Any Caller to Grief a Victim's Pending Deposit UTXO - (File: contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/refund.rs)

### Summary
`request_refund` in `satoshi-bridge` has no restriction on who can call it. Any unprivileged actor can submit a refund request for any UTXO — including a victim's pending deposit — by supplying the victim's publicly observable `deposit_msg` and an arbitrary `refund_address`. If `execute_refund` is called after the timelock before the relayer finalizes `verify_deposit`, the UTXO is permanently marked in `verified_deposit_utxo`, blocking the deposit and redirecting the victim's BTC.
