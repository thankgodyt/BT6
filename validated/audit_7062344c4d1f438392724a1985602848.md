### Title
Chain Selection at Forecast Horizon Uses Empty Peras Weight Snapshot Instead of Authoritative State - (File: ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/MiniProtocol/ChainSync/Client.hs)

### Summary

`checkPreferTheirsOverOurs` in the ChainSync client decides whether to disconnect from a peer whose header is beyond the forecast horizon by calling `preferAnchoredCandidate` with a hardcoded `emptyPerasWeightSnapshot` instead of the live `PerasWeightSnapshot` maintained by the `PerasCertDB`. This is the direct analog of the `balanceOf` vs `getReserves()` pattern: a zero/empty value is read instead of querying the authoritative protocol state. When Peras is active, this causes the node to make incorrect chain-preference decisions at the forecast boundary, potentially disconnecting from peers offering the canonical (heavier) chain and remaining on a lighter, non-canonical chain.
