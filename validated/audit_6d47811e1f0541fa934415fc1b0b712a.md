Audit Report

## Title
`claim_fee` Gated by `#[trusted_relayer]` Permanently Locks Fast-Transfer Fees After Relayer Resignation — (`near/omni-bridge/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary

`claim_fee` is the sole on-chain mechanism for a relayer to collect fees from fast transfers whose final destination is a non-NEAR chain (EVM, Solana, etc.). The function is decorated with `#[trusted_relayer]`, which rejects callers who are not currently in the trusted-relayer set. `resign_trusted_relayer` immediately removes the relayer and returns their stake with no check for outstanding fee claims, so any relayer who resigns (or is revoked) before calling `claim_fee` permanently forfeits the fee portion of tokens they legitimately fronted.

## Finding Description

**Fast-transfer-to-other-chain flow.** When a relayer calls `fast_fin_transfer` with a non-NEAR recipient, `fast_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` (lib.rs L914–973) is invoked. It burns/locks the relayer's fronted tokens, records a `FastTransferStatus` in `fast_transfers`, and writes a `TransferMessage` to `pending_transfers` with `origin_transfer_id = Some(fast_transfer.transfer_id)` (L956). The fee is embedded in `transfer_message.amount` and is only disbursed later via `claim_fee`.

**`claim_fee` guard.** `claim_fee` (L1054–1064) carries `#[trusted_relayer]`, which checks the caller's current membership in the trusted-relayer set at call time. A resigned relayer fails this check immediately.

**`claim_fee_callback` exclusivity.** Inside `claim_fee_callback` (L1083–1086), the contract enforces `fee_recipient == predecessor_account_id`. No third party can call `claim_fee` on behalf of the resigned relayer; the check is hard-coded to the caller's identity.

**`resign_trusted_relayer` has no pending-fee guard.** The integration test (relayer_staking.rs L338–368) confirms that `resign_trusted_relayer` removes the relayer from the trusted set and returns the stake unconditionally, with no inspection of `pending_transfers` or `fast_transfers`.

**No alternative recovery path.** `process_fin_transfer_to_near` (L1867–1978) automatically routes the full amount (including fee) to the relayer when the first leg finalises for NEAR-destination fast transfers, but this path is not taken for non-NEAR destinations. `process_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` (L1980–2054) sends only `amount_without_fee` to the relayer (L2032–2038) and does not disburse the fee. No other public function removes the `TransferMessage` from `pending_transfers` and pays the fee to the relayer.

## Impact Explanation

The fee portion of the bridged amount is permanently frozen inside the bridge contract. The relayer fronted real tokens (burned/locked on NEAR, forwarded to the destination chain), and the fee that was supposed to compensate them has no reachable disbursement path once trusted status is lost. This constitutes permanent freezing of bridged funds, matching the Critical impact class.

## Likelihood Explanation

Any active trusted relayer can trigger this unilaterally: execute a fast transfer to an EVM/Solana address, then call `resign_trusted_relayer` before the destination chain finalises and before `claim_fee` is called. The window between fast-transfer execution and fee claim can span hours or days (the destination chain must finalise first). A relayer exiting the system for legitimate reasons may not realise pending fees are forfeited. The exploit requires no external cooperation, no oracle manipulation, and no privileged role beyond being a trusted relayer.

## Recommendation

In `resign_trusted_relayer`, before removing the relayer's trusted status, either:
1. Revert if the relayer has any `pending_transfers` entries whose `origin_transfer_id` points to a fast transfer owned by this relayer (i.e., where `fast_transfers` records them as the relayer), or
2. Relax the `#[trusted_relayer]` guard on `claim_fee` to also permit callers who are the `fee_recipient` recorded in the on-chain proof, regardless of current trusted status, so that legitimately earned fees remain claimable after resignation.
