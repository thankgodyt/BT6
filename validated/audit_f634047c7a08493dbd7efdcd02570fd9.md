### Title
Stale Supply Snapshot in Token Migration Allows Holder to Burn Old Tokens and Still Receive New Tokens — (File: `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/nbtc/migration.rs`)

---

### Summary

`migrate_to_new_token` snapshots `ft_total_supply` and every holder's `ft_balance_of` in a single parallel batch, then validates and mints in a later callback. Between the snapshot receipt and the callback receipt, any token holder can call `storage_unregister(force: true)` on the `nbtc` contract, burning their balance and reclaiming their storage deposit. Because both `total_supply` and `sum` were captured at the same instant, the `sum == total_supply` guard still passes, and the migration mints the pre-burn balance to the attacker on the new token — producing a new-token supply that exceeds the old token's final supply.

---
