### Title
DAO-Only Access Control on `withdraw_protocol_fee` Creates Single Point of Failure for Protocol Fee Extraction - (File: `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/api/management.rs`)

---

### Summary

The `withdraw_protocol_fee` function is exclusively gated behind `#[access_control_any(roles(Role::DAO))]`. Protocol fees (nBTC tokens) continuously accumulate in the bridge contract from every deposit and withdrawal. If DAO access is lost or all DAO keys are compromised, the entire accumulated protocol fee balance is permanently locked in the bridge contract with no recovery path.

---

### Finding Description

`withdraw_protocol_fee` in `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/api/management.rs` is the **sole mechanism** to extract protocol fees from the bridge:

```rust
#[payable]
#[access_control_any(roles(Role::DAO))]
pub fn withdraw_protocol_fee(&mut self, amount: Option<U128>) -> Promise {
    assert_one_yocto();
    let total_protocol_fee = self.data().cur_available_protocol_fee;
    let amount = amount.map_or(total_protocol_fee, |v| v.0);
    require!(amount > 0 && amount <= total_protocol_fee, "Invalid amount");
    self.data_mut().cur_available_protocol_fee -= amount;
    self.data_mut().acc_claimed_protocol_fee += amount;
    self.internal_withdraw_protocol_fee(amount)
}
``` [1](#0-0) 

The underlying transfer in `internal_withdraw_protocol_fee` sends fees to `env::predecessor_account_id()` — the DAO caller — not a fixed treasury address:

```rust
pub fn internal_withdraw_protocol_fee(&self, amount: u128) -> Promise {
    ext_ft_core::ext(self.internal_config().nbtc_account_id.clone())
        ...
        .ft_transfer(env::predecessor_account_id(), amount.into(), None)
``` [2](#0-1) 

Protocol fees accumulate in `cur_available_protocol_fee` from two sources:

1. **Deposits** — credited in `mint_callback` on every successful `verify_deposit`: [3](#0-2) 

2. **Withdrawals** — credited in `verify_withdraw_burn_callback` on every successful `verify_withdraw`: [4](#0-3) 

The `ContractData` struct confirms `cur_available_protocol_fee` is the live balance of extractable fees: [5](#0-4) 

There is **no alternative path** to extract these fees. No other function touches `cur_available_protocol_fee` in a way that releases it to an external recipient. The DAO role is the exclusive and irreplaceable gatekeeper.

---

### Impact Explanation

If all DAO key holders lose access (key loss, governance failure, or a DAO account being deleted on NEAR), the entire `cur_available_protocol_fee` balance of nBTC tokens is permanently locked inside the bridge contract. The nBTC tokens remain in the bridge's account on the nBTC contract but can never be transferred out. This constitutes permanent locking of protocol funds — a Medium impact matching: *"Harmful smart-contract behavior without direct funds theft, including … stuck bridge state requiring operator intervention."*

Unlike the Clober case where fees went to fixed addresses (making permissionless triggering safe), here the fee destination is `env::predecessor_account_id()`, so the access control cannot simply be removed — but the DAO remains a single point of failure with no fallback.

---

### Likelihood Explanation

Low-to-medium. NEAR accounts can be deleted, keys can be lost, and multi-sig governance can deadlock. The bridge is designed for long-term operation, and protocol fees accumulate continuously with every bridge transaction. Over time, the locked amount grows. The absence of any recovery mechanism (no secondary role, no fixed treasury address, no time-locked fallback) means a single governance failure permanently destroys the accumulated fee balance.

---

### Recommendation

Introduce a fixed, immutable `protocol_fee_treasury` address in the bridge `Config`. Modify `internal_withdraw_protocol_fee` to always transfer to that fixed address rather than `env::predecessor_account_id()`. Once the destination is fixed, the access control on `withdraw_protocol_fee` can be relaxed (e.g., callable by anyone, or at minimum by `Operator` in addition to `DAO`) so that DAO is no longer a single point of failure for fee delivery.

---

### Proof of Concept

1. Bridge operates normally; every `verify_deposit` and `verify_withdraw` call credits `cur_available_protocol_fee` with nBTC tokens held by the bridge contract.
2. DAO key(s) are lost (e.g., hardware wallet destroyed, NEAR account key rotated incorrectly, governance multisig deadlocked).
3. No account holds `Role::DAO`; `withdraw_protocol_fee` reverts for every caller.
4. `cur_available_protocol_fee` continues to grow with each bridge operation but can never be extracted.
5. All accumulated protocol fees (nBTC) are permanently locked in the bridge contract with no on-chain recovery path. [1](#0-0) [6](#0-5)
