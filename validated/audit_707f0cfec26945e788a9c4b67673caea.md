### Title
Attacker Can Pre-empt Victim's `request_refund` by Submitting a Competing Refund Request, Temporarily Locking Bridged Funds — (File: `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/refund.rs`)

---

### Summary

`request_refund` has no depositor-ownership check. Any NEAR account can submit a refund request for any UTXO by supplying the victim's publicly-emitted `deposit_msg` and a valid Bitcoin Merkle proof. Because `request_refund_callback` enforces a one-request-per-UTXO invariant, the attacker's earlier call pre-populates `refund_requests[utxo_storage_key]` and causes every subsequent legitimate call by the real depositor to revert with `"Refund request already exists for this UTXO"`.
