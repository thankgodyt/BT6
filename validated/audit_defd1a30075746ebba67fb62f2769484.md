### Summary

The degenerate `BlockSupportsPeras` instance — the only instance currently in the codebase — implements `validatePerasCert` as a stub that unconditionally returns `Right` for every certificate, skipping all cryptographic and semantic checks. Certificates received from any peer are accepted without validation and their weight boost is immediately applied to the `PerasWeightSnapshot` used by chain selection. This is the direct analog of the external report's asymmetric-enforcement pattern: the constraint (certificate legitimacy) is enforced on the **output** side (chain selection uses the weight) but is entirely absent on the **input** side (certificate ingestion), allowing an unprivileged peer to inject arbitrary weight boosts and steer chain selection toward a non-canonical chain.
