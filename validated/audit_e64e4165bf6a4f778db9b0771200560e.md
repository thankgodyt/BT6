Audit Report

## Title
Detached Fee `ft_transfer` in `fin_transfer_send_tokens_callback` Causes Permanent Escrow Mis-Accounting — (File: `near/omni-bridge/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary

In `fin_transfer_send_tokens_callback`, the fee payment to the relayer for non-deployed (custodied) tokens is dispatched via `ft_transfer(...).detach()`, a fire-and-forget call whose result is never observed. Because `locked_tokens` was already decremented by the full transfer amount (principal + fee) in `process_fin_transfer_to_near` before any token movement, any failure of the detached `ft_transfer` — whether a panic in a standard NEP-141 token or a silent return in a non-standard one — leaves the fee tokens permanently stranded in the bridge contract while the accounting ledger records them as gone. `FinTransferEvent` is emitted unconditionally, falsely signaling complete success.

## Finding Description

**Step 1 — `process_fin_transfer_to_near` (L1881–1885):** `unlock_tokens_if_needed` is called with `transfer_message.amount.0` — the full amount including the fee — permanently decrementing `locked_tokens` before any cross-contract token movement occurs.

**Step 2 — Principal delivery (L1957–1977):** `send_tokens` dispatches `amount_without_fee` to the recipient, chained to `fin_transfer_send_tokens_callback`. This leg has a proper callback and can revert via `revert_lock_actions` on failure.

**Step 3 — Fee delivery (L1727–1733):** Inside the success branch of `fin_transfer_send_tokens_callback`, the fee portion for non-deployed tokens is sent via:

```rust
ext_token::ext(token)
    .with_attached_deposit(ONE_YOCTO)
    .with_static_gas(FT_TRANSFER_GAS)
    .ft_transfer(fee_recipient.clone(), transfer_message.fee.fee, None)
    .detach();
```

`.detach()` schedules the promise but discards its result entirely. If the `ft_transfer` panics (standard NEP-141 failure — e.g., fee recipient's storage unregistered between the earlier `check_storage_balance_result` at L1919–1933 and this callback), the token contract's state reverts but the bridge's state — including the `locked_tokens` decrement — does not revert. The fee tokens remain in the bridge contract with no accounting entry.

**Step 4 — Unconditional event emission (L1745):** `FinTransferEvent` is emitted immediately after the detached call, regardless of whether the fee transfer succeeded or failed.

The storage balance check at L1919–1933 runs during `process_fin_transfer_to_near`, before `send_tokens` executes. The callback runs in a separate NEAR receipt after `send_tokens` completes, creating a window during which the fee recipient can unregister storage, causing the `ft_transfer` to panic and the fee to be stranded. No non-standard token behavior is required; a standard NEP-141 panic is sufficient because the result is detached.

## Impact Explanation

This is a concrete escrow mis-accounting and fee mis-accounting impact matching the critical allowed scope. `locked_tokens` is decremented by `amount` (principal + fee), but only `amount_without_fee` exits the bridge; the `fee` portion remains held by the bridge contract with no corresponding ledger entry. The bridge's internal accounting permanently diverges from its actual token balance. The stranded fee tokens are unrecoverable through any normal bridge operation. `FinTransferEvent` falsely records the fee as delivered, causing off-chain relayer infrastructure and indexers to record incorrect state. The DAO can adjust `locked_tokens` via `set_locked_tokens` but cannot extract the stranded tokens without a dedicated rescue function.

## Likelihood Explanation

The trigger requires only that the detached `ft_transfer` fail, which can occur via: (1) the fee recipient unregistering their NEP-141 storage account between the storage-balance check in `process_fin_transfer_to_near` and the callback execution — a window that exists across two NEAR receipts and is exploitable by any fee recipient; or (2) any non-standard custodied token whose `ft_transfer` does not panic on failure. Custodied tokens are external contracts not controlled or audited by the bridge. The exploit is reachable by any relayer or bridge user who initiates a transfer with a non-zero fee using an affected token, and is repeatable across every such transfer.

## Recommendation

Replace the detached fee `ft_transfer` with a chained callback that observes the result. If the fee transfer fails, the callback must re-lock the fee amount in `locked_tokens` to restore accounting consistency:

```rust
ext_token::ext(token)
    .with_attached_deposit(ONE_YOCTO)
    .with_static_gas(FT_TRANSFER_GAS)
    .ft_transfer(fee_recipient.clone(), transfer_message.fee.fee, None)
    .then(
        Self::ext(env::current_account_id())
            .with_static_gas(FEE_TRANSFER_CALLBACK_GAS)
            .fee_transfer_callback(token, fee_amount, chain_kind),
    );
```

The `fee_transfer_callback` should check `env::promise_result(0)` and, on failure, call `lock_tokens` to restore the fee amount in `locked_tokens`, preventing the permanent accounting discrepancy. The `FinTransferEvent` should only be emitted after confirming the fee transfer succeeded.

## Proof of Concept

1. Register a custodied (non-deployed) NEP-141 token with the bridge.
2. A user on a foreign chain locks `1000` units with a fee of `100` units; the relayer submits the proof via `fin_transfer`.
3. `process_fin_transfer_to_near` runs: `unlock_tokens_if_needed` decrements `locked_tokens` by `1000` (L1881–1885).
4. `send_tokens` delivers `900` units to the recipient. Between this receipt and the callback receipt, the fee recipient calls `storage_unregister` on the token contract.
5. `fin_transfer_send_tokens_callback` fires. `ft_transfer(relayer, 100, None).detach()` is called. The token contract panics (storage not registered); the token contract's state reverts, but the bridge's state does not. The `100` fee tokens remain in the bridge.
6. `FinTransferEvent` is emitted at L1745, indicating full success.
7. `locked_tokens` reads `0` for this token/chain pair, but the bridge holds `100` untracked tokens. The discrepancy is permanent and unrecoverable through normal bridge operations.
