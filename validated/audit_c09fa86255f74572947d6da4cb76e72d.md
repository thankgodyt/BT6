Audit Report

## Title
Fee-on-Transfer Token Escrow Mis-Accounting in `initTransfer` — (File: `evm/src/omni-bridge/contracts/OmniBridge.sol`)

## Summary
`OmniBridge.initTransfer` calls `safeTransferFrom` with the caller-supplied `amount` but takes no balance snapshot, so for fee-on-transfer tokens the bridge receives `amount − fee` while emitting `InitTransfer` with the full `amount`. The NEAR side mints the full `amount` of wrapped tokens, permanently under-collateralising the escrow by the fee delta on every deposit. On redemption, `finTransfer` releases the full `payload.amount`, draining reserves funded by other depositors.

## Finding Description
`logMetadata` carries no access-control modifier, so any external caller can register any ERC-20 — including fee-on-transfer tokens — with the NEAR side of the bridge: [1](#0-0) 

In `initTransfer`, the plain-ERC-20 branch (reached when `customMinters[tokenAddress] == address(0)` and `!isBridgeToken[tokenAddress]`) performs: [2](#0-1) 

No `balanceOf` snapshot is taken before or after the call. The function then unconditionally emits: [3](#0-2) 

`amount` here is the caller-supplied parameter, not the actual tokens received. The `InitTransfer` event is the authoritative signal consumed by the NEAR relayer to mint wrapped tokens: [4](#0-3) 

On the redemption path, `finTransfer` releases the full `payload.amount` from the bridge's ERC-20 reserves: [5](#0-4) 

For a token with transfer tax `t`, the bridge holds `amount × (1 − t)` after deposit but must pay out `amount` on redemption. The shortfall `amount × t` is silently taken from other depositors' reserves, making the bridge progressively insolvent for that token.

## Impact Explanation
This is a concrete instance of **escrow mis-accounting** — an explicitly listed Critical impact class. The bridge locks less collateral than it credits cross-chain. After enough round-trips the bridge cannot honour redemptions for honest users who deposited standard amounts of the same token, constituting a direct, quantifiable, permanent loss of bridged funds. No admin interaction is required; the loss accrues automatically on every deposit/redemption cycle.

## Likelihood Explanation
Medium-to-High. The attacker needs only: (1) a fee-on-transfer ERC-20 (deployable permissionlessly or an existing one such as tokens with configurable transfer fees); (2) a call to the permissionless `logMetadata`; (3) repeated calls to `initTransfer` and `finTransfer`. No privileged key, oracle, or threshold-signature compromise is required. The entry path is fully unprivileged and repeatable.

## Recommendation
Snapshot the bridge's balance before and after `safeTransferFrom` and use the delta — not the caller-supplied `amount` — in all downstream accounting and in the emitted event:

```solidity
uint256 balanceBefore = IERC20(tokenAddress).balanceOf(address(this));
IERC20(tokenAddress).safeTransferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), amount);
uint256 actualReceived = IERC20(tokenAddress).balanceOf(address(this)) - balanceBefore;
// replace `amount` with `actualReceived` in initTransferExtension call and InitTransfer emit
```

Apply the same pattern to the `customMinters` branch where `safeTransferFrom` targets the minter address.

## Proof of Concept
1. Deploy `FeeToken` — a standard ERC-20 that deducts 5% on every `transferFrom`.
2. Call `OmniBridge.logMetadata(address(FeeToken))` — no permission required; emits `LogMetadata`.
3. Wait for the NEAR relayer to process `LogMetadata` and register the wrapped asset.
4. Call `OmniBridge.initTransfer(address(FeeToken), 1_000e18, 0, 0, "near:attacker.near", "")`:
   - `safeTransferFrom` moves `1_000e18` from attacker; bridge receives `950e18` (5% fee).
   - `InitTransfer` emits `amount = 1_000e18`.
   - NEAR mints `1_000e18` wrapped tokens to `attacker.near`.
5. Bridge back `1_000e18` wrapped tokens from NEAR; MPC signs `finTransfer` payload with `amount = 1_000e18`:
   - Bridge calls `safeTransfer(attacker, 1_000e18)` against a reserve of only `950e18`.
   - `50e18` is silently taken from other depositors' funds (or the call reverts if no other funds exist, causing DoS for all redeemers).
6. Repeat steps 4–5 to drain the bridge's entire reserve for `FeeToken`.
