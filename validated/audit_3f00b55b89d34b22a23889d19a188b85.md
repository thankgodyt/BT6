Audit Report

## Title
Fee Tokens Permanently Locked When `fee_recipient` Relayer Is Revoked Before Calling `claim_fee` - (File: `near/omni-bridge/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary
`claim_fee` enforces two independent caller restrictions: a `#[trusted_relayer]` guard and an explicit `fee_recipient == predecessor_account_id` check in the callback. If the relayer designated as `fee_recipient` during `sign_transfer` is subsequently revoked by the DAO, neither the revoked relayer nor any other account can satisfy both restrictions simultaneously. The fee portion of every affected transfer is permanently locked in `pending_transfers` and `locked_tokens` is never decremented.

## Finding Description
The NEAR→Foreign transfer flow embeds `fee_recipient` into an MPC-signed `TransferMessagePayload` at `sign_transfer` time, making it cryptographically immutable after the fact. [1](#0-0) 

`claim_fee` is guarded by `#[trusted_relayer]` at the method level: [2](#0-1) 

The impl-level `#[trusted_relayer]` macro grants `bypass_roles` only to `Role::DAO` and `Role::UnrestrictedRelayer`, allowing them to skip the relayer-status check: [3](#0-2) 

However, `claim_fee_callback` independently enforces that the caller must be exactly the `fee_recipient` from the proof: [4](#0-3) 

After revocation:
- The revoked relayer fails the `#[trusted_relayer]` check (no longer active).
- The DAO or any other trusted relayer bypasses the relayer-status check but fails the `fee_recipient == predecessor_account_id` check because their account ID does not match the embedded `fee_recipient`.
- No account can satisfy both restrictions simultaneously.
- `remove_transfer_message` at line 1094 is never reached, so `pending_transfers` retains the entry and `locked_tokens` is never decremented for the fee amount. [5](#0-4) 

There is no admin rescue function, no DAO override path, and no mechanism to administratively clear a stuck `TransferMessage`.

## Impact Explanation
The fee portion of every NEAR→Foreign transfer whose `fee_recipient` relayer is revoked before `claim_fee` is called is permanently frozen inside the bridge contract. `locked_tokens` retains a stale inflated value, creating a permanent discrepancy between the bridge's accounting and its actual spendable balance. This constitutes both permanent freezing of bridged funds and fee mis-accounting, both of which are explicitly within the Critical impact scope.

## Likelihood Explanation
The DAO legitimately revokes relayers for misbehavior via `reject_relayer_application`, and relayers can voluntarily resign via `resign_trusted_relayer`. Any relayer that has signed one or more transfers with a non-zero fee and has not yet called `claim_fee` will have those fees permanently locked the moment revocation occurs. No attacker is required; this is a natural consequence of normal protocol administration. The scenario is repeatable across any number of transfers and any number of revoked relayers.

## Recommendation
Remove the `#[trusted_relayer]` guard from `claim_fee` (or add a specific bypass for the `fee_recipient` account regardless of current relayer status), so that the designated `fee_recipient` can always claim their earned fee even after losing trusted-relayer status. Alternatively, allow the DAO to administratively clear a stuck `pending_transfers` entry and transfer the locked fee to a configurable rescue address, ensuring `locked_tokens` is correctly decremented.

## Proof of Concept
1. User calls `ft_transfer_call` → `init_transfer` with `amount = 10000`, `fee = 1000`. `pending_transfers` stores the `TransferMessage`.
2. Trusted relayer `relayer.near` calls `sign_transfer(transfer_id, fee_recipient="relayer.near", fee=1000)`. MPC signature is produced; destination chain finalizes, sending `9000` tokens to the user. `1000` tokens remain locked on NEAR.
3. DAO calls `reject_relayer_application("relayer.near")`. `relayer.near` is no longer a trusted relayer.
4. `relayer.near` calls `claim_fee(...)` → panics at `#[trusted_relayer]`: relayer is not active.
5. Any currently-active trusted relayer calls `claim_fee(...)` → passes `#[trusted_relayer]` but panics at `BridgeError::OnlyFeeRecipientCanClaim` because `fee_recipient ("relayer.near") != predecessor_account_id`.
6. DAO calls `claim_fee(...)` → bypasses `#[trusted_relayer]` via `bypass_roles` but panics at `BridgeError::OnlyFeeRecipientCanClaim` because the DAO account is not `"relayer.near"`.
7. The `TransferMessage` with `fee = 1000` tokens remains in `pending_transfers` indefinitely. `locked_tokens` is never decremented. The `1000` tokens are permanently frozen.
