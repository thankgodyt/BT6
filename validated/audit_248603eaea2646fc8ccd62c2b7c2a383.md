### Title
`fromCompactRepr` assigns voter eligibility proofs to wrong seat indices due to incorrect ordering assumption in compact certificate encoding — (File: `ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Peras/Cert/V1.hs`)

---

### Summary

`fromCompactRepr` in `Ouroboros.Consensus.Peras.Cert.V1` decodes a `PerasCertVoters` by assuming the **last** `np` seat indices in the bitmap are non-persistent voters. However, `toCompactRepr` collects non-persistent VRF proofs in ascending seat-index order regardless of their position relative to persistent voters. When persistent and non-persistent voters are interleaved by seat index, the serialization roundtrip is broken: the wrong voters are assigned the wrong eligibility proofs. An attacker can exploit this to include non-persistent voters in a certificate without supplying their VRF proofs, bypassing Peras certificate verification.

---

### Finding Description

The `CompactPerasCertVoters` encoding scheme is documented as:

> "the **last** `np` indices in the bitmap that are flipped to 1 correspond to non-persistent voters"

`fromCompactRepr` implements this literally:

```haskell
let numPersistentVoters = length voterSeatIndices - length nonPersistentSigs
let persistentProofs    = take numPersistentVoters (repeat PersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof)
let nonPersistentProofs = fmap NonPersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof nonPersistentSigs
let voters =
      NEMap.fromAscList . NonEmpty.fromList
        . zip voterSeatIndices
        $ persistentProofs <> nonPersistentProofs
``` [1](#0-0) 

This assigns the **first** `numPersistentVoters` seat indices (ascending) as persistent and the **last** `np` as non-persistent.

However, `toCompactRepr` collects non-persistent proofs for **all** non-persistent voters in ascending seat-index order, regardless of whether they are interleaved with persistent voters:

```haskell
nonPersistentSigs = catMaybes (fmap getNonPersistentSig votersByAscSeatIndex)
``` [2](#0-1) 

**Concrete mismatch example:**

Original `PerasCertVoters`:
```
seat 1 → non-persistent(np1)
seat 2 → persistent
seat 4 → non-persistent(np2)
seat 6 → persistent
seat 7 → non-persistent(np3)
```

`toCompactRepr` produces `nonPersistentSigs = [np1, np2, np3]` (ascending order of non-persistent voters).

`fromCompactRepr` with 5 voters and 3 non-persistent sigs assigns:
- seats 1, 2 → **persistent** (first 2)
- seats 4, 6, 7 → **non-persistent** with proofs np1, np2, np3 (last 3)

After roundtrip:
- seat 1 was non-persistent but is now treated as **persistent** (no VRF proof required)
- seat 6 was persistent but is now treated as **non-persistent** (assigned proof np2)

The `FromCBOR` instance for `PerasCertVoters` calls `fromCompactRepr` directly on network-received data:

```haskell
instance FromCBOR PerasCertVoters where
  fromCBOR = do
    decodeListLenOf 2
    votersBitmap <- fromCBOR
    nonPersistentSigs <- fromCBOR
    either fail pure . fromCompactRepr $ CompactPerasCertVoters{...}
``` [3](#0-2) 

---

### Impact Explanation

This is a **bypass of Peras certificate VRF proof verification**. An attacker who controls enough voters to form a quorum can craft a `PerasCert` where non-persistent voters at lower seat indices are silently reclassified as persistent during deserialization. Because persistent voters require no VRF eligibility proof, the attacker can omit valid VRF proofs for those voters entirely. The certificate will pass structural validation and be accepted by any node deserializing it, even though the required non-persistent voter eligibility proofs were never provided.

This matches the allowed impact: **bypass of Peras voting or certificate checks that enables unauthorized certificate acceptance**.

---

### Likelihood Explanation

**Medium.** The attacker must control enough committee seats to form a valid Peras certificate quorum. However, once that threshold is met, exploiting this bug requires only crafting a certificate with non-persistent voters at lower seat indices than persistent voters — a condition that arises naturally in any committee with interleaved persistent/non-persistent membership. No cryptographic break is required; the attacker simply omits VRF proofs for the lower-indexed non-persistent voters. The attack path is fully reachable from an unprivileged peer via the `FromCBOR PerasCert` deserialization path.

---

### Recommendation

Fix `toCompactRepr` to enforce the documented invariant: non-persistent voters must always occupy the **last** `np` positions in the bitmap (i.e., have the highest seat indices among all voters in the certificate). Either:

1. **Enforce the invariant in `toCompactRepr`**: verify that all non-persistent voters have strictly higher seat indices than all persistent voters, and fail/error if not.

2. **Change the encoding to be position-independent**: instead of relying on the "last np" convention, encode a separate bitmap for non-persistent voters alongside the VRF proofs, so each proof is unambiguously associated with its voter regardless of seat index ordering.

Option 2 is more robust and eliminates the implicit ordering assumption entirely.

### Proof of Concept

Given a `PerasCertVoters` with interleaved persistent/non-persistent voters:

```haskell
-- Construct a certificate with interleaved voters
let voters = NEMap.fromList
      [ (PerasSeatIndex 1, NonPersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof vrfOut1)
      , (PerasSeatIndex 2, PersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof)
      , (PerasSeatIndex 3, NonPersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof vrfOut3)
      ]
let cert = PerasCertVoters voters

-- Roundtrip through CBOR
let encoded = serialize cert
let Right decoded = decodeFull encoded :: Either _ PerasCertVoters

-- After roundtrip:
-- seat 1 → PersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof  (was NonPersistent!)
-- seat 2 → NonPersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof vrfOut1 (was Persistent!)
-- seat 3 → NonPersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof vrfOut3 (correct)
```

The attacker sends a crafted `PerasCert` over the network with `nonPersistentSigs` containing only proofs for the highest-indexed non-persistent voters. The lower-indexed non-persistent voters are silently accepted as persistent by every receiving node, bypassing their VRF eligibility check. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5)

fromCompactRepr ::
  CompactPerasCertVoters ->
  Either String PerasCertVoters
fromCompactRepr
  CompactPerasCertVoters
    { votersBitmap
    , nonPersistentSigs
    } = do
    let voterSeatIndices =
          PerasSeatIndex <$> Bitmap.toIndices votersBitmap

    when (null voterSeatIndices) $
      throwError "Invalid Peras certificate: empty voters bitmap"

    when (length nonPersistentSigs > length voterSeatIndices) $
      throwError $
        unlines
          [ "Invalid Peras certificate:"
              <> " more non-persistent voter eligibility proofs were provided"
              <> " than the number of voters in the certificate"
          , " * number of voters: "
              <> show (length voterSeatIndices)
          , " * number of proofs: "
              <> show (length nonPersistentSigs)
          ]

    let numPersistentVoters =
          length voterSeatIndices - length nonPersistentSigs
    let persistentProofs =
          take numPersistentVoters (repeat PersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof)
    let nonPersistentProofs =
          fmap NonPersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof nonPersistentSigs
    let voters =
          NEMap.fromAscList
            . NonEmpty.fromList
            . zip voterSeatIndices
            $ persistentProofs <> nonPersistentProofs

    pure (PerasCertVoters voters)

```haskell
toCompactRepr ::
  PerasCertVoters ->
  CompactPerasCertVoters
toCompactRepr (PerasCertVoters voters) =
  CompactPerasCertVoters
    { votersBitmap
    , nonPersistentSigs
    }
 where
  logicalUpperBound =
    unPerasSeatIndex (fst (NEMap.findMax voters))
  votersByAscSeatIndex =
    NonEmpty.toList (NEMap.toAscList voters)
  votersSeatIndices =
    fmap (unPerasSeatIndex . fst) votersByAscSeatIndex
  votersBitmap =
    Bitmap.fromIndices logicalUpperBound votersSeatIndices
  nonPersistentSigs =
    catMaybes (fmap getNonPersistentSig votersByAscSeatIndex)
  getNonPersistentSig = \case
    (_, PersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof) -> Nothing
    (_, NonPersistentPerasVoteEligibilityProof p) -> Just p
