Peras Vote Deduplication Bypass via `implGarbageCollect` Resetting `pvdsVoteIds` - (File: `ouroboros-consensus/src/ouroboros-consensus/Ouroboros/Consensus/Storage/PerasVoteDB/Impl.hs`)

### Summary

`implAddVote` uses `Set.member voteId (pvdsVoteIds pvds)` as the **sole guard** preventing a voter from voting more than once per round. `implGarbageCollect` unconditionally removes those same vote IDs from `pvdsVoteIds` when a round is garbage-collected. After GC, the identical `(roundNo, voterId)` pair is absent from the set, so the deduplication check passes and the vote is re-accepted as if it were fresh. This is the direct structural analog of the BullvBear bug: a "has-been-processed" sentinel is cleared by a separate state-management operation, allowing a once-only action to be repeated.
