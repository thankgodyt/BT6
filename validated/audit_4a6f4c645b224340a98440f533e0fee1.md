### Title
`reject_refund` Does Not Cancel Pre-Existing `BTCPendingInfo`, Allowing Attacker to Complete Refund After DAO Rejection — (File: `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/refund.rs`)

### Summary
The DAO's emergency `reject_refund` mechanism removes only the `RefundRequest` record but leaves any `BTCPendingInfo` created by a prior `execute_refund` call intact. An attacker who frontrunned the DAO's rejection by calling `execute_refund` the moment the timelock expired can still obtain MPC signatures and complete the BTC transfer to their own address, rendering the rejection ineffective.

### Finding Description
When `execute_refund` is called, three things happen in `finalize_refund_with_psbt`:

1. A `BTCPendingInfo` is created with state `Refund(PendingSign)` and stored under the caller's account
2. The deposit UTXO is inserted into `verified_deposit_utxo`
3. The `RefundRequest` is kept with `executed = true`

When the DAO subsequently calls `reject_refund`, `internal_reject_refund` only removes the `RefundRequest` from `refund_requests`. It does **not** remove the `BTCPendingInfo`, does **not** clean up `btc_pending_sign_ids`, and does **not** remove the UTXO from `verified_deposit_utxo`.

After rejection, the attacker's `BTCPendingInfo` remains in `PendingSign` state. The attacker can call `sign_btc_transaction` to obtain MPC signatures, broadcast the signed BTC transaction, and then call `verify_refund_finalize` to finalize. The `verify_refund_finalize_callback` explicitly handles the case where the `RefundRequest` was already removed — treating it as a harmless no-op — so it succeeds even after rejection.

The attack is specifically enabled for deposits where `deposit_msg.refund_address` is `None` (the "unsafe" refund path). In this case, any NEAR account can submit `request_refund` with an arbitrary BTC address. After `unsafe_refund_timelock_sec` (14 days by default) elapses, the attacker calls `execute_refund` to create the `BTCPendingInfo`. Even if the DAO then calls `reject_refund`, the `BTCPendingInfo` persists and the attacker can complete the theft.
