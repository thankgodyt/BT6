### Title
`env::signer_account_id()` Used for Relayer Fee Attribution Enables Front-Running Theft of Relayer Fees - (File: `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/nbtc/mint.rs`, `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/nbtc/burn.rs`, `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/btc_light_client/deposit.rs`)

---

### Summary

The bridge uses `env::signer_account_id()` (NEAR's analog of Solidity's `tx.origin`) instead of `env::predecessor_account_id()` (`msg.sender`) to attribute relayer fees and storage deposit refunds. This allows an attacker to front-run a legitimate relayer's transaction and redirect the relayer fee and storage deposit to themselves.

---

### Finding Description

In three separate locations, the bridge records the relayer identity using `env::signer_account_id()`:

**1. Deposit minting (`mint.rs`, line 25):**
```rust
ext_nbtc::ext(self.internal_config().nbtc_account_id.clone())
    .with_static_gas(GAS_FOR_MINT_CALL)
    .mint(
        recipient_id.clone(),
        mint_amount,
        protocol_fee,
        env::signer_account_id(),   // ← relayer_account_id
        relayer_fee,
        post_actions,
    )
```

**2. Withdrawal burn (`burn.rs`, lines 22 and 39):**
```rust
.burn(
    btc_pending_info.account_id.clone(),
    btc_pending_info.burn_amount.into(),
    env::signer_account_id(),   // ← relayer_account_id
    relayer_fee.into(),
)
```

**3. Safe deposit callback (`deposit.rs`, lines 429, 448, 453):**
```rust
let relayer_account_id = env::signer_account_id();
// ...
.burn(env::current_account_id(), mint_amount, relayer_account_id, U128(0))
// ...
Promise::new(env::signer_account_id())
    .transfer(self.required_balance_for_safe_deposit())  // storage deposit refund
    .detach();
```

In NEAR Protocol, `env::signer_account_id()` is the original key-holder who signed the transaction — equivalent to Solidity's `tx.origin`. `env::predecessor_account_id()` is the direct caller — equivalent to `msg.sender`. When a relayer operates through a smart contract wallet (e.g., a DAO multisig, a proxy, or any contract-based account), `signer_account_id` is the individual key-holder who submitted the transaction, **not** the smart contract wallet that should receive the fee.

An attacker who observes a pending `verify_deposit` or `verify_withdraw` transaction in the NEAR mempool can copy the calldata and submit it from their own account first (with higher gas priority). Because the relayer fee recipient is determined by `signer_account_id` at the time of the callback, the attacker's account becomes the fee recipient.

---

### Impact Explanation

- The relayer fee (deducted from the deposit amount) is paid to the attacker instead of the legitimate relayer.
- In the `safe_verify_deposit` flow, the storage deposit (attached NEAR tokens) is also refunded to `env::signer_account_id()`, meaning the attacker recovers the storage cost while the legitimate relayer loses it.
- The user's nBTC minting is unaffected — `recipient_id` comes from `deposit_msg`, not from `signer_account_id`. There is no unauthorized minting or loss of user funds.
- This constitutes a medium-severity impact: attacker-triggered redirection of relayer fees and storage deposits, without direct theft of bridged assets.

---

### Likelihood Explanation

- NEAR smart contract wallets and DAO multisigs are common for protocol relayers.
- The NEAR mempool is observable; front-running is feasible.
- The attack requires no special privileges — any NEAR account can submit the same calldata.
- The attack is repeatable for every deposit or withdrawal proof submission made by a smart-contract-wallet relayer.

---

### Recommendation

Replace all uses of `env::signer_account_id()` used for relayer fee attribution with `env::predecessor_account_id()`. For callbacks (which are `#[private]` and called by the contract itself), the relayer identity must be captured at the public entry point and passed explicitly through the callback chain, just as `recipient_id` is already passed through.

For example, in `internal_mint_promise`, accept a `relayer_id: AccountId` parameter captured at the public entry point using `env::predecessor_account_id()`, and thread it through to `mint()` and `mint_callback()`.

---

### Proof of Concept

1. Legitimate relayer `relayer.near` (a multisig contract) has a key-holder `key-holder.near` submit a `verify_deposit` call on its behalf. The transaction is visible in the NEAR mempool.
2. Attacker `attacker.near` copies the exact calldata and submits it with higher gas, front-running `key-holder.near`.
3. The bridge's light-client verification succeeds (the proof is valid regardless of who submits it).
4. In `verify_deposit_callback`, `internal_mint_promise` is called with `env::signer_account_id()` = `attacker.near` as the relayer.
5. The nBTC contract's `mint()` pays the relayer fee to `attacker.near`.
6. `relayer.near` receives no fee despite having prepared and intended to submit the proof. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2)
