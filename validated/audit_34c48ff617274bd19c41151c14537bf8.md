### Title
No Cancellation Mechanism for Refund `BTCPendingInfo` in `PendingSign` State Causes Permanent Stuck Bridge State - (File: contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/refund.rs)

---

### Summary

The `execute_refund` function creates a `BTCPendingInfo` entry in `PendingInfoStage::PendingSign` state and simultaneously marks the deposit UTXO in `verified_deposit_utxo`. Unlike withdrawals (which have `cancel_withdraw`) and UTXO management (which has `cancel_active_utxo_management`), there is no cancellation mechanism for a refund pending info while its refund request is still active. If MPC signing consistently fails or is never completed, the refund is permanently stuck: re-execution is blocked by a duplicate-ID guard, and the only cleanup path (`remove_refund_pending_tx_id`) is gated on the refund request being absent. Resolving the stuck state requires operator intervention, and even after intervention the deposit UTXO remains permanently blocked in `verified_deposit_utxo`, preventing both future deposits and future refund requests for that UTXO.
