Audit Report

## Title
Resigned Relayer Cannot Claim Earned Fast-Transfer Fees — (`near/omni-bridge/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary

`claim_fee` is guarded by `#[trusted_relayer]`, which rejects callers who are not currently in the trusted-relayer set. `resign_trusted_relayer` immediately removes the relayer and returns their stake with no check for pending fee claims. For fast transfers whose destination is a non-NEAR chain (EVM, Solana, etc.), the fee is stored in `pending_transfers` and is only recoverable via `claim_fee`. A relayer who resigns before calling `claim_fee` permanently loses those fee tokens, which remain locked in the bridge contract with no alternative recovery path.

## Finding Description

**Fast transfer to non-NEAR destination flow:**

When a relayer executes a fast transfer to a non-NEAR destination, `fast_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` is called. It burns/locks `amount_without_fee` and creates a new `TransferMessage` with `origin_transfer_id: Some(fast_transfer.transfer_id.clone())` stored in `pending_transfers`. The fee portion is embedded in `transfer_message.fee` and is not disbursed at this point. [1](#0-0) 

When the first leg finalizes (origin chain → NEAR), `process_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` sends `amount_without_fee` back to the relayer but does **not** disburse the fee — that remains in the `pending_transfers` entry. [2](#0-1) 

The only code path that disburses the fee is `claim_fee` → `claim_fee_callback` → `send_fee_internal`. [3](#0-2) 

`claim_fee` is decorated with `#[trusted_relayer]`, which panics if the caller is not currently in the trusted-relayer set. [4](#0-3) 

`claim_fee_callback` additionally enforces `fee_recipient == predecessor_account_id`, blocking any third party from claiming on behalf of the resigned relayer. [5](#0-4) 

`resign_trusted_relayer` (exercised in integration tests) immediately removes the relayer from the trusted set and returns their stake with no check for outstanding entries in `pending_transfers` or `fast_transfers`. [6](#0-5) 

The `bypass_roles(Role::DAO, Role::UnrestrictedRelayer)` on the impl block allows those roles to bypass the `#[trusted_relayer]` guard on `claim_fee`, but the inner `fee_recipient == predecessor_account_id` check in `claim_fee_callback` still blocks them from claiming on behalf of the resigned relayer, since their account ID differs from the recorded `fee_recipient`. [7](#0-6) 

## Impact Explanation

The fee tokens (real bridged assets such as USDC) fronted by the relayer are permanently frozen inside the bridge contract. There is no alternative withdrawal path: `remove_transfer_message` for EVM/Solana-destination transfers is only reachable through `claim_fee_callback`, which requires the caller to be both a current trusted relayer and the recorded `fee_recipient`. This constitutes permanent freezing of bridged funds, matching the Critical allowed impact class.

## Likelihood Explanation

Any active trusted relayer can trigger this unilaterally by calling `resign_trusted_relayer` after executing a fast transfer to a non-NEAR destination but before calling `claim_fee`. The window between fast-transfer execution and fee claim can span hours to days (the second leg must finalize on the destination chain and the first-leg proof must be submitted). The relayer may resign for legitimate operational reasons without realizing pending fees are forfeited. No external attacker cooperation is required; the relayer controls both actions entirely through public contract calls.

## Recommendation

In `resign_trusted_relayer`, before removing the relayer's trusted status and returning their stake, the contract should either:
1. Revert if the relayer has any `pending_transfers` entries where `origin_transfer_id` is `Some` and the associated `fast_transfers` entry records this relayer as the fee recipient, or
2. Relax the `#[trusted_relayer]` guard on `claim_fee` to also permit callers who are the recorded `fee_recipient` in the submitted proof, regardless of current trusted-relayer status (while keeping all other validation in `claim_fee_callback` intact).

## Proof of Concept

1. Relayer stakes ≥ 1,000 NEAR and becomes trusted via `apply_for_trusted_relayer`.
2. Relayer calls `ft_on_transfer` with a `FastFinTransferMsg` targeting an EVM address, fronting tokens to the user. `fast_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` stores a `TransferMessage` (with `origin_transfer_id` set) in `pending_transfers` and a `FastTransferStatus` in `fast_transfers`.
3. Relayer calls `resign_trusted_relayer`. Stake is returned; relayer is removed from the trusted set. No check for pending fee claims occurs.
4. The first leg (origin EVM → NEAR) finalizes via `fin_transfer`; `process_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` sends `amount_without_fee` to the relayer. The fee entry remains in `pending_transfers`.
5. The second leg (NEAR → destination EVM) finalizes and emits a `FinTransfer` event with `fee_recipient = relayer`.
6. Relayer attempts `claim_fee` with the EVM proof. The `#[trusted_relayer]` macro panics because the relayer is no longer trusted.
7. No other account can call `claim_fee` for this transfer: the `fee_recipient == predecessor_account_id` check in `claim_fee_callback` blocks it.
8. The fee tokens are permanently locked in the bridge contract.
