### Title
Detached `burn()` Call in `safe_mint_callback` Silently Fails, Permanently Inflating nBTC Supply - (File: `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/btc_light_client/deposit.rs`)

---

### Summary

In `safe_mint_callback`, when `safe_mint` fails to deliver tokens to the recipient (e.g., recipient account not registered), the bridge issues a cleanup `burn()` cross-contract call with `.detach()` and **no `.then()` callback**. If this burn fails silently, the tokens already minted to the bridge's own balance are never destroyed, permanently inflating the nBTC total supply beyond the amount of BTC locked in the bridge.

---

### Finding Description

The `safe_verify_deposit` flow proceeds as follows:

1. `verify_safe_deposit_callback` calls `safe_mint(recipient_id, mint_amount, msg)` on the nbtc contract.
2. Inside `safe_mint`, tokens are **unconditionally minted to `bridge_id`** first: [1](#0-0) 

3. If the recipient account is not registered, `safe_mint` returns `U128(0)` immediately, leaving the minted tokens sitting in the bridge's balance.
4. Back in `safe_mint_callback`, `is_refund_required()` detects the `U128(0)` result and enters the failure branch. The bridge removes the UTXO from `verified_deposit_utxo` and then issues a cleanup burn: [2](#0-1) 

5. The `burn()` call is dispatched with `.detach()` and **no `.then()` callback**. `GAS_FOR_BURN_CALL` is only 5 TGas: [3](#0-2) 

6. If the burn cross-contract call fails for any reason (gas exhaustion, nbtc contract panic, etc.), the failure is silently discarded. No state rollback occurs in the bridge contract because the callback does not exist.

The result: `verified_deposit_utxo` no longer tracks the UTXO (it was removed at line 441), the UTXO is not added to the bridge's available set, but the minted tokens remain permanently in the bridge's balance — nBTC total supply now exceeds the BTC locked in the bridge.

---

### Impact Explanation

The nBTC token supply becomes unbacked. The bridge's own balance holds tokens that correspond to no locked BTC. These tokens can participate in future bridge operations — for example, they are drawn upon when `burn()` is called during legitimate withdrawals: [4](#0-3) 

This means a future legitimate withdrawal could consume these phantom tokens instead of the user's legitimately transferred tokens, breaking the 1:1 BTC-backing invariant and corrupting protocol accounting. This is a broken-callback-rollback / stuck-accounting scenario matching the Medium impact class.

---

### Likelihood Explanation

The trigger condition — a recipient account not being registered on NEAR — is a routine user scenario, not an exotic edge case. Any user who calls `safe_verify_deposit` (or any Omni Bridge integration that routes to `safe_verify_deposit`) with an unregistered recipient account will reach this code path. The burn failure probability is elevated by the tight 5 TGas gas budget allocated to the burn call, which must perform storage reads/writes and event emission on the nbtc contract.

---

### Recommendation

Replace the fire-and-forget `.detach()` burn with a chained callback that detects failure and either retries or records the orphaned amount for operator recovery. For example:

```rust
ext_nbtc::ext(self.internal_config().nbtc_account_id.clone())
    .with_static_gas(GAS_FOR_BURN_CALL)
    .burn(env::current_account_id(), mint_amount, relayer_account_id, U128(0))
    .then(
        Self::ext(env::current_account_id())
            .with_static_gas(GAS_FOR_SAFE_MINT_BURN_CALLBACK)
            .safe_mint_burn_callback(mint_amount),
    );
```

The `safe_mint_burn_callback` should check `is_promise_success()` and, on failure, record the orphaned amount in a recoverable ledger (similar to the existing `lost_found` pattern used in `transfer_nbtc_callback`): [5](#0-4) 

---

### Proof of Concept

1. Attacker (or ordinary user) sends BTC to a deposit address derived from a `DepositMsg` whose `recipient_id` is a NEAR account that has **never registered storage** on the nbtc contract.
2. A relayer calls `safe_verify_deposit(...)` with a valid Merkle proof.
3. `verify_safe_deposit_callback` fires → calls `safe_mint(unregistered_account, amount, msg)`.
4. `safe_mint` executes `self.token.internal_deposit(&self.bridge_id, amount.into())` — tokens are minted to bridge balance — then immediately returns `U128(0)` because `self.token.accounts.get(&account_id).is_none()`.
5. `safe_mint_callback` enters the `else` branch: removes the UTXO from `verified_deposit_utxo`, then calls `burn(...).detach()` with 5 TGas.
6. If the burn cross-contract call panics (e.g., gas exhaustion), the failure is silently dropped.
7. **Result**: nBTC `total_supply` is permanently inflated by `amount` satoshis with no corresponding locked BTC. The bridge's own `ft_balance_of(bridge_id)` is elevated by `amount`, and future `burn()` calls during legitimate withdrawals will draw from this phantom balance. [6](#0-5) [1](#0-0)
