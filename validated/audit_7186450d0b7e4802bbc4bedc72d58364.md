Audit Report

## Title
Detached `send_tokens` Promise After Irrevocable `mark_fast_transfer_as_finalised` Causes Permanent Relayer Fund Loss — (File: `near/omni-bridge/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary

In `utxo_fin_transfer_fast`, when the destination chain is not NEAR, the contract irrevocably sets `finalised = true` in persistent storage before dispatching the relayer reimbursement as a detached (fire-and-forget) promise. If that promise fails for any reason, the fast transfer record is permanently finalised and the relayer has no protocol-level path to recover the tokens they burned or locked when executing the fast transfer. The same pattern exists in `process_fin_transfer_to_other_chain`. The developers explicitly flagged this gap with a `TODO` comment at the call site.

## Finding Description

**`utxo_fin_transfer_fast` (non-NEAR destination branch):**

At line 2533, `mark_fast_transfer_as_finalised` writes `finalised = true` to contract storage immediately and unconditionally: [1](#0-0) 

At lines 2542–2548, `send_tokens` is called with `.detach()`, discarding the promise result entirely: [2](#0-1) 

`mark_fast_transfer_as_finalised` persists the state change synchronously in the current call frame: [3](#0-2) 

NEAR's execution model commits all state mutations from the current call frame regardless of what detached child promises do. If the detached `send_tokens` promise fails (token contract panics, `ft_transfer` reverts, insufficient gas allocated to the child), `finalised = true` persists. The guard at lines 2524–2527 then permanently blocks any retry: [4](#0-3) 

The developers explicitly acknowledged this is unresolved with a `TODO` at the call site: [5](#0-4) 

**`process_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` (non-UTXO path):**

The same pattern exists here — `send_tokens(...).detach()` is called at lines 2029–2039, followed immediately by `mark_fast_transfer_as_finalised` at line 2040, with no callback to handle failure: [6](#0-5) 

**Root cause of the relayer's fund loss:**

During `fast_fin_transfer_to_other_chain`, the relayer's tokens are burned (for deployed tokens) or locked (for non-deployed tokens) at line 932: [7](#0-6) 

The sole recovery mechanism for those burned/locked tokens is the reimbursement dispatched in `utxo_fin_transfer_fast` or `process_fin_transfer_to_other_chain`. If that detached promise fails silently, the relayer's advance is permanently unrecoverable.

## Impact Explanation

The relayer burned or locked their own bridged tokens to advance the transfer to the recipient. If the detached `send_tokens` promise fails, those tokens are permanently lost or frozen with no protocol-level retry path. This constitutes **permanent loss or freezing of bridged funds** belonging to the relayer — a concrete match to the Critical allowed impact: *"Stealing, loss, double-spending, unauthorized minting, or permanent freezing of bridged funds across NEAR, EVM, Solana, Starknet, Bitcoin, Zcash, or Wormhole-routed flows."*

## Likelihood Explanation

Concrete failure triggers for the detached `send_tokens`:

1. **Token contract panic on `ft_transfer` or `mint`** — any panic in the token contract is swallowed by `.detach()`. Token contract upgrades, bugs, or edge-case reverts (e.g., recipient storage not registered at the time of reimbursement) silently drop the reimbursement.
2. **Gas exhaustion of the child promise** — `send_tokens` computes remaining gas dynamically at lines 2063–2067. If the parent call frame consumed most of the prepaid gas before reaching `send_tokens`, the child promise may receive insufficient gas and fail silently.
3. **Relayer storage deregistration** — for non-deployed tokens, `ft_transfer` requires the recipient (the relayer) to have a storage deposit. If the relayer's storage registration was removed between fast transfer execution and finalization, `ft_transfer` panics and the detached promise fails silently.

The `TODO` comment at line 2484 is an explicit in-code admission that the failure path has no resolution and is an open risk. [8](#0-7) 

## Recommendation

Replace the fire-and-forget pattern in both `utxo_fin_transfer_fast` and `process_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` with a chained callback that checks `env::promise_result(0)` on failure and either reverts `finalised` to `false` (allowing a retry) or records a pending reimbursement that DAO/admin can settle:

```rust
self.send_tokens(
    fast_transfer.token_id.clone(),
    fast_transfer_status.relayer.clone(),
    amount,
    "",
)
.then(
    Self::ext(env::current_account_id())
        .with_static_gas(RESOLVE_UTXO_FAST_TRANSFER_GAS)
        .resolve_utxo_fast_transfer_reimbursement(
            &fast_transfer.id(),
            fast_transfer_status.relayer,
            amount,
        ),
)
```

In the callback, on failure, revert `finalised` to `false` or emit a recoverable pending-reimbursement record. Apply the same fix to `process_fin_transfer_to_other_chain` at lines 2029–2040. Remove the `TODO` comment only after this is implemented.

## Proof of Concept

1. Deploy a mock NEP-141 token that panics on `ft_transfer` when called with a specific recipient address.
2. Register the mock token in the bridge and configure a UTXO chain connector.
3. Have a trusted relayer call `fast_fin_transfer` for a BTC→EVM transfer; the relayer's tokens are burned at line 932.
4. Trigger `ft_on_transfer` from the UTXO connector with a `UtxoFinTransfer` message matching the same transfer ID.
5. `utxo_fin_transfer` detects the existing fast transfer status and calls `utxo_fin_transfer_fast`.
6. `mark_fast_transfer_as_finalised` sets `finalised = true` at line 2533 — state is committed.
7. `send_tokens(...).detach()` dispatches the mock token's `ft_transfer`, which panics; the detached promise fails silently.
8. **Assert**: `get_fast_transfer_status` returns `finalised = true`; relayer token balance is unchanged (reimbursement never arrived).
9. **Assert**: Any retry of the finalization call fails with `FastTransferAlreadyFinalised`.

The relayer's advance is permanently lost with no protocol-level recovery path.
