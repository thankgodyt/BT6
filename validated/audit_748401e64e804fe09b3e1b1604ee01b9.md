### Title
Publicly Callable `clear_invalid_pending_verify_rbf` Removes `BTCPendingInfo` Without Cleaning Account Tracking Sets — (File: `contracts/satoshi-bridge/src/api/bridge.rs`)

---

### Summary

`clear_invalid_pending_verify_rbf` and `batch_clear_invalid_pending_verify_rbf` are callable by any unprivileged NEAR account. Their internal implementation removes a `BTCPendingInfo` entry from contract storage but does **not** remove the corresponding entry from the owning account's `btc_pending_verify_list`, leaving the account in a permanently inconsistent state. The analog vulnerability class is: publicly reachable state-manipulation function with no access control and incomplete validation/cleanup, mirroring the Atlas `rollback_*` pattern.

---
